Professional Guarding

Guarding That Thinks, Verifies And Responds

Mamba combines professional guarding, smart access control, LPR alerts, live patrol checkpoints and control-centre oversight into one managed security operation for estates, road closures, schools and businesses across the East Rand.

Let's Talk Guarding Explore Guarding Services
Access control intelligence

Every entry, exit and patrol becomes part of the security picture

Modern guarding is not a guard at a gate with a clipboard. It is a managed access ecosystem where trained officers are supported by visitor messaging, RFID, boom barriers, LPR alerts, live patrol checks and real-time escalation.

WhatsApp and SMS Invites

Guests receive clear visit details before arrival, including access conditions, instructions and the information needed for a smoother gate experience.

Entry and Exit PINs

Personalised access PINs can be configured for once-off use or limited validity, improving estate integrity without frustrating approved visitors.

RFID and Boom Barriers

Approved vehicles can be identified automatically, keeping traffic moving while maintaining strict control over who enters high-traffic sites.

LPR Alerts

Automatic plate recognition reads vehicles day or night, checks databases, triggers alerts and records time, date and direction for investigations.

Live Patrol System

Checkpoint scanning confirms patrol movement and immediately alerts the control centre if a checkpoint is missed or a concern is raised.

Google Maps Guidance

Visitor routes and location links reduce confusion at the gate and help contractors, guests and service providers arrive prepared.

Operational backbone

The guard on site is backed by a full response machine

Mamba guarding is supported by control-centre infrastructure, dedicated technical teams, armed response coverage, medical-response partnerships and fire-response escalation through Fire Ops SA.

Operational support Control-centre resources, smart cameras and response teams support agreed site procedures.
Guarding built around the site

Professional security officers, site-specific procedures and measurable oversight

Each solution is shaped around the premises, the people moving through it and the risks that matter most. The result is visible security backed by selection, training, management inspections and fast response support.

01

Corporate Guarding

Corporate guarding starts with a site risk assessment, then turns into a tailored plan covering access control, patrol routes, reporting, escalation and the technologies needed to protect the premises properly.

Client-specific Standard Operating Procedures are built with your team, and officers are trained against those procedures before deployment. Selection includes background checks, psychometric testing and emotional stability assessment so the right officers are matched to the right environment.

  • Risk assessment and custom SOPs
  • Access control, patrols and technology integration
  • Guarding manager oversight, meetings and inspections
